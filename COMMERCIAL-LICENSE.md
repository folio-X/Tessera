# Tessera Commercial License

The Tessera index data and methodology are published by FolioX Inc. under [CC-BY-NC 4.0](./LICENSE-DATA) — free for non-commercial use, with a separate commercial license required for commercial use.

This document describes what commercial use is, and how to license it.

## What counts as non-commercial (free)

The free CC-BY-NC 4.0 license covers, for example:

- Academic research and publications
- Journalism, including paywalled outlets, when Tessera is cited as a reference (not redistributed as data)
- Internal personal use (a developer comparing model prices for their side project)
- Contributions to open-source projects that are themselves non-commercial
- Educational use in courses and tutorials

In all cases, attribution to "Tessera, FolioX Inc." is required.

## What counts as commercial (requires a license)

A separate commercial license is required for any of the following:

- **Derivatives reference use** — using a Tessera value as the underlying or settlement price for a derivative, swap, forward, future, or option
- **Listed-product use** — using a Tessera value as the reference for a listed financial product (ETF, ETN, structured note)
- **Enterprise data subscription** — internal use of Tessera data at a for-profit company with annual revenue above $10M
- **Fintech platform integration** — embedding Tessera values into a commercial product, service, or dashboard sold to third parties
- **Redistribution** — redistributing Tessera data as part of a paid product, whether bundled with other data or standalone
- **AI-pricing data product** — operating a competing pricing index or aggregator whose data is derived from Tessera

If you're not sure whether your intended use is commercial, ask: tessera@foliox.ai. We will give you a written answer in writing within five business days.
